This course was taken in the University of Michigan, Fall 2021; it was taught by Professor [Paolo Squatriti](https://lsa.umich.edu/history/people/faculty/pasqua.html), who is really a talented and good lecturer. It discusses the history of Western Europe from 235 when the 3rd Century Crisis emerged, to the Norman Conquest in 1066. It partially talks about Eastern Empire and the Islamic World, which were also considered as inherits of legacies of Roman Empire.

It is a very typical course talking about the early Middle Ages, there is ([a course offered by Yale](https://oyc.yale.edu/NODE/171)) with similar structures. It is also the first course of the sequence of HISTORY 210-211, which covers the whole period (235-1492). I also went to listen HISTORY 211 in the subsequent semester but did not take that course. In fact, if you learn enough in 210, you will have the ability to study 211 by yourself.

這是我於2021年秋在密歇根大學上的歷史課, 由[Paolo Squatriti](https://lsa.umich.edu/history/people/faculty/pasqua.html)教授開設. 講述的是從235年三世紀危機出現, 直到1066諾曼征服英格蘭的西歐歷史, 部分涉及東帝國和伊斯蘭世界 (這三者都被視作羅馬帝國遺產的繼承者). 

實際上內容和耶魯的那個 ([The Early Middle Ages](https://oyc.yale.edu/NODE/171)) 幾乎一樣; 後來我也把耶魯這個聽完了. 這門課屬於比較經典的中世紀歷史入門課, 也是HISTORY 210-2
